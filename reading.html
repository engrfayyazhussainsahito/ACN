Reading list

Every reading in the course, by week. Items marked in the first position of each week are the essential ones.

Week 1: Internet Architecture and Design Principles

  • Clark (1988) The design philosophy of the DARPA Internet protocols.
  • Saltzer, Reed, Clark (1984) End-to-end arguments in system design.
  • Kurose & Ross, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach, ch. 1.

Week 2: Queueing Theory and Performance Modelling

  • Paxson & Floyd (1995) Wide-area traffic: the failure of Poisson modeling.
  • Kleinrock, Queueing Systems Vol. 1.
  • Appenzeller, Keslassy, McKeown (2004) Sizing router buffers.

Week 3: Routing: Link-State, Distance-Vector and BGP

  • Gao & Rexford (2001) Stable Internet routing without global coordination.
  • Labovitz et al. (2000) Delayed Internet routing convergence.
  • RIPE NCC (2008) YouTube hijacking: a RIPE NCC RIS case study.

Week 4: Congestion Control I: AIMD, Reno and CUBIC

  • Jacobson (1988) Congestion avoidance and control.
  • Chiu & Jain (1989) Analysis of the increase and decrease algorithms.
  • Ha, Rhee, Xu (2008) CUBIC: a new TCP-friendly high-speed TCP variant.

Week 5: Congestion Control II: BBR, Delay-Based and Modelling

  • Mathis, Semke, Mahdavi, Ott (1997) The macroscopic behavior of the TCP congestion avoidance algorithm.
  • Cardwell et al. (2016) BBR: congestion-based congestion control. ACM Queue.
  • Nichols & Jacobson (2012) Controlling queue delay.

Week 6: Modern Transport: QUIC, TLS 1.3 and Multipath

  • Langley et al. (2017) The QUIC transport protocol: design and Internet-scale deployment. SIGCOMM.
  • RFC 9000 (QUIC) and RFC 8446 (TLS 1.3).
  • Raiciu et al. (2012) How hard can it be? Designing and implementing a deployable multipath TCP.
  • RFC 9001: Using TLS to secure QUIC.
  • RFC 9002: QUIC loss detection and congestion control.
  • RFC 9114 (HTTP/3), RFC 9204 (QPACK) and RFC 9218 (Extensible priorities).
  • Kakhki et al. (2017) Taking a long look at QUIC. IMC.
  • Yu et al. (2021) Dissecting performance of production QUIC. The Web Conference.

Week 7: Data Center Networks

  • Al-Fares, Loukissas, Vahdat (2008) A scalable, commodity data center network architecture.
  • Greenberg et al. (2009) VL2.
  • Alizadeh et al. (2010) Data center TCP (DCTCP).

Week 8: Midterm Review and Exam

  • Re-read the Essential papers of Weeks 1 to 7.

Week 9: SDN and Programmable Data Planes

  • McKeown et al. (2008) OpenFlow: enabling innovation in campus networks.
  • Bosshart et al. (2014) P4: programming protocol-independent packet processors.
  • Cormode & Muthukrishnan (2005) An improved data stream summary: the count-min sketch.
  • Jain et al. (2013) B4.
  • Bloom (1970) Space/time trade-offs in hash coding with allowable errors. CACM.
  • Broder & Mitzenmacher (2004) Network applications of Bloom filters: a survey.
  • Kazemian, Varghese, McKeown (2012) Header space analysis. NSDI.
  • Fogel et al. (2015) A general approach to network configuration analysis (Batfish). NSDI.
  • Katta et al. (2016) HULA: scalable load balancing using programmable data planes. SOSR.
  • Jin et al. (2017) NetCache: balancing key-value stores with fast in-network caching. SOSP.
  • Sapio et al. (2021) Scaling distributed machine learning with in-network aggregation. NSDI.

Week 10: Wireless and Mobile Networking

  • Shannon (1948) A mathematical theory of communication.
  • Tse & Viswanath, Fundamentals of Wireless Communication.
  • Heusse et al. (2003) Performance anomaly of 802.11b.

Week 11: Content Delivery and Video Streaming

  • Breslau et al. (1999) Web caching and Zipf-like distributions.
  • Huang et al. (2014) A buffer-based approach to rate adaptation. SIGCOMM.
  • Mao, Netravali, Alizadeh (2017) Neural adaptive video streaming with Pensieve.

Week 12: Network Security: DDoS, DNS, TLS and BGP

  • Rossow (2014) Amplification hell: revisiting network protocols for DDoS abuse.
  • RFC 6811 (BGP prefix origin validation), RFC 2827 (BCP 38).
  • Cloudflare (2018) Memcached DDoS attack write-ups.

Week 13: Measurement, Satellite Networks and the Future

  • Handley (2018) Delay is not an option: low latency routing in space. HotNets.
  • Bajpai & Schönwälder (2015) A survey on Internet performance measurement platforms.
  • Sundaresan et al. (2011) Broadband Internet performance: a view from the gateway. SIGCOMM.

Week 14: AI Cluster Networking: RDMA, Collectives and Rail Design

  • Patarasuk & Yuan (2009) Bandwidth optimal all-reduce algorithms for clusters of workstations. JPDC.
  • Thakur, Rabenseifner, Gropp (2005) Optimization of collective communication operations in MPICH.
  • Zhu et al. (2015) Congestion control for large-scale RDMA deployments (DCQCN). SIGCOMM.
  • Guo et al. (2016) RDMA over commodity Ethernet at scale. SIGCOMM.
  • Gangidi et al. (2024) RDMA over Ethernet for distributed AI training at Meta scale. SIGCOMM.
  • Shoeybi et al. (2019) Megatron-LM: training multi-billion parameter language models using model parallelism.
  • Narayanan et al. (2021) Efficient large-scale language model training on GPU clusters using Megatron-LM. SC.
  • Rajbhandari et al. (2020) ZeRO: memory optimizations toward training trillion parameter models. SC.
  • Jouppi et al. (2023) TPU v4: an optically reconfigurable supercomputer for machine learning. ISCA.
  • Mittal et al. (2015) TIMELY: RTT-based congestion control for the datacenter. SIGCOMM.
  • Li et al. (2019) HPCC: high precision congestion control. SIGCOMM.
  • Montazeri et al. (2018) Homa: a receiver-driven low-latency transport protocol. SIGCOMM.
  • Handley et al. (2017) Re-architecting datacenter networks and stacks for low latency and high performance (NDP). SIGCOMM.
  • Daly (2006) A higher order estimate of the optimum checkpoint interval for restart dumps. FGCS.

Week 15: WAN Traffic Engineering, MPLS, Overlays and Naming

  • Rosen, Viswanathan, Callon (2001) RFC 3031: Multiprotocol label switching architecture.
  • Jain et al. (2013) B4: experience with a globally-deployed software defined WAN. SIGCOMM.
  • Hong et al. (2013) Achieving high utilization with software-driven WAN (SWAN). SIGCOMM.
  • Stoica et al. (2001) Chord: a scalable peer-to-peer lookup service. SIGCOMM.
  • Maymounkov & Mazières (2002) Kademlia: a peer-to-peer information system based on the XOR metric.
  • Filsfils et al. (2018) RFC 8402: Segment routing architecture.

Week 16: Final Projects, Review and Exam

  • Reproduce one SIGCOMM/NSDI paper figure with Mininet or ns-3.
